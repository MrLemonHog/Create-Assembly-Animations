## Assembly and disassembly of a [Create Aeronautics](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-aeronautics) ship finally look like something

### The physics assembler no longer works in silence: you watch it go over your build while it becomes a ship, and the same on disassembly

<br>

![What this mod do?](https://github.com/MrLemonHog/archive/blob/main/img/apt/wtmdv2.png?raw=true)

## Pick a style for assembly and another for disassembly:

### **Diagram** - the structure is redrawn as a page of the Contraption Diagram: a pencil sketches the outline, the blocks turn into its dithered grey palette, and the centre of mass, dimensions and force arrows.

<img src="https://github.com/MrLemonHog/archive/blob/main/img/caa/showcase.webp?raw=true" width="100%" alt="diagram style">
<br>
<br>

### **Scanner** - a laser sheet sweeps the structure: up on assembly, down on disassembly.

<img src="https://github.com/MrLemonHog/archive/blob/main/img/caa/scan.webp?raw=true" width="100%" alt="scanner style">
<br>
<br>

## Set up separately

Assembly and disassembly each get their own switch and style. Every style has its own page with brightness, speed
and options of its own: force arrows and measurements for Diagram, laser colour and halo for Scanner. Each tile shows the style running, so you see what you pick.
